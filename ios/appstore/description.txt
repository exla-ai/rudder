Rudder runs your coding agents (Claude Code and Codex) on a cloud machine and puts them in your pocket: read their conversations, answer their questions, approve changes, start new tasks and merge work from your phone.

See every project and agent at a glance. When an agent has a question, needs an approval or has a plan ready, Rudder puts it at the top and shows a badge, so you can answer in a few taps and let it carry on.

From your phone you can:
- Read each agent's conversation as it works, and send it a message.
- Answer questions, approve actions and approve plans.
- Start a new agent on any project and pick its model.
- Attach photos, screenshots and files to a message.
- Talk to Rudder: ask what your agents are doing, or tell them what to do, by voice.
- Sign Claude Code or Codex in on your cloud machine without a laptop.
- Get a morning digest of what your agents did since yesterday.
- Check your cloud machine, its workspaces and your plan usage.

Rudder also has a Mac app and a command line tool. They stay in sync: start an agent on your laptop and follow it on your phone.

Rudder is free and open source. You sign in with Google or GitHub, and the agents use your own Claude or ChatGPT plan.
