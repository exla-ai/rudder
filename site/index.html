Claude Code or Codex. Anywhere.

It's the same harness you already use, on your existing Claude or ChatGPT subscription. No API keys, no extra plan. It just runs on a machine that stays on, so you can pick it up from wherever you are.

Run it anywhere.

Sign in once and every surface sees the same projects and the same agents. Start a task in the terminal, check on it from any browser or the Mac, answer it from your phone.

  1. 01 · terminal

    The dashboard, in your terminal

    Every agent in its own pane, with its diff and the merge a key away. rudder cloud runs the same dashboard on your cloud machine.

    $ npm i -g @exla/rudder
    then run rudder · macOS, Linux · Node 22+
  2. 02 · web

    Rudder in your browser

    The same agent and plan panels as the Mac app, in any browser. Nothing to install: sign in and your projects are there.

    Open the web app Sign in with Google or GitHub
  3. 03 · mac app

    Rudder for Mac

    The Rudder app in its own window, a menu bar count of what needs you, and one click to pull a cloud agent onto your Mac.

    Download for Mac Signed and notarized · macOS 14+
  4. 04 · phone

    Rudder on your iPhone

    Start tasks, answer prompts and send photos or files. Or just talk to your agents out loud. CarPlay is coming.

Close the laptop. The agents keep going.

They run on your own cloud machine, and your phone is the remote. Start work from the coffee line, answer the prompt that would have stalled it, and ship the result as a draft PR without opening the laptop.

  1. 01

    Start agents from the phone

    Type a task into any project. /model picks Claude or Codex. Dev servers an agent starts show up as links you can open right there.

  2. 02

    Approve prompts as they come

    Permission requests, questions and plans land on your phone. Allow, deny with a reason, answer, or text the agent anything mid-turn. The terminal still works too; whichever answers first wins.

  3. 03

    Pick up on any computer

    rudder login prints a short code. Approve it on your phone and that terminal is signed in, with every project and agent where you left it.

For solo developers and teams.

Use it on your own, or bring your team. Everyone keeps their own machine and their own subscription.

  • On your own

    Sign in and go. Your cloud machine, your repos, your agents. Nothing to set up.

  • With a team

    Make an org and invite people with a link. See what everyone's agents are working on, keep any agent private, and share Slack and Linear across the org. You can be in more than one org.
