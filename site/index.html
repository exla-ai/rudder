Rudder in 36 seconds.

Close the laptop. The agents keep going.

They run on your own cloud machine, and your phone is the remote. Start work from the coffee line, answer the prompt that would have stalled it, and ship the result as a draft PR without opening the laptop.

  1. 01

    Start agents from the phone

    Type a task into any project. /model picks Claude or Codex. Dev servers an agent starts show up as links you can open right there.

  2. 02

    Approve prompts as they come

    Permission requests, questions and plans land on your phone. Allow, deny with a reason, answer, or text the agent anything mid-turn. The terminal still works too; whichever answers first wins.

  3. 03

    Pick up on any computer

    rudder login prints a short code. Approve it on your phone and that terminal is signed in, with every project and agent where you left it.
