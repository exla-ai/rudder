Rudder

Support

Help with Rudder on iPhone, Mac, the web and the command line. Rudder is made by Exla.

Get help

Ask a question or report a problem at github.com/exla-ai/rudder/issues. We read every issue. Say which app you use (iPhone, Mac, web or CLI), its version from Settings, and what you did before the problem appeared.

The guides are at rudder.exla.ai/docs.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in on iPhone? Use Continue with Apple, Google or GitHub. If you already use Rudder on a computer, sign in the same way to see the same projects and agents.
  • My agents will not start. Agents run on your own Claude or ChatGPT plan. Open Settings and sign Claude Code or Codex in on your cloud machine.
  • The app says the cloud machine is waking. A machine with no running agents sleeps to save cost and takes up to a minute to wake.
  • How do I delete my account? Open Settings and choose Delete account, under Sign out. This removes your sign-in, your cloud machine and everything stored on it.
  • What does Rudder collect? See the privacy policy.
